Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Live Clipper ("the app") is a personal application used only by its owner to upload video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what the app does with data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Upload permission for the owner's YouTube channel, used only to upload the owner's clips.
  • Read access to the owner's channel, used only to check whether uploaded clips were processed, rejected or removed.

The app does not access, collect, store or share data about any other YouTube user.

How data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sent to any server other than Google's. The app keeps a local log of what it uploaded (video ID, title and date). No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the app's local files removes all stored data.
